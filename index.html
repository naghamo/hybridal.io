Train Smarter, Not Harder Switching Signal-Guided Training in Active Learning

Nagham Omar · Maya Rozenshtein · Evgeny Mishlyakov · Avigdor Gal

Whether to retrain from scratch or fine-tune from the last checkpoint is an overlooked decision in active learning. HybridAL watches an online stabilization signal and switches once, keeping macro-F1 within a 0.010 margin of both strategies while cutting training time by up to 49%.
